📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Dashboard for Sales Analysis

Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance, revenue, product trends, and regional performance.

Business Problem

The company needed an interactive dashboard to monitor sales performance, identify top-selling products, track regional sales, and support better business decisions.

Dataset

  • 5000 Sales Orders
  • Product Information
  • Customer Details
  • Revenue Data
  • Regional Sales

Key Insights

  • Revenue exceeded ₹120 million.
  • South region generated the highest sales.
  • Electronics was the top-performing category.
  • Sales increased during the final quarter.

Business Recommendations

  • Increase marketing in the South region.
  • Expand Electronics inventory.
  • Improve sales in low-performing regions.
